How It Works

Simple and transparent — from idea to finished artwork.

  1. Send Your Idea Share reference images, character descriptions, or a mood board.
  2. Get a Quote We'll discuss details and I'll provide a final price.
  3. Confirm & Pay Deposit 50% deposit to start. Full payment before final delivery.
  4. Sketch & Revisions You'll see the sketch first. Minor revisions are included.
  5. Final Delivery High-resolution PNG/PSD file delivered to you.

Commission Guidelines

  • Pricing varies based on complexity, number of characters, and background detail.
  • A 50% deposit is required before work begins. Remaining 50% due before final delivery.
  • Up to 2 minor revisions are included at no extra cost.
  • Major changes after the sketch phase may incur additional fees.
  • No refunds once the sketch has been approved and coloring has started.
  • Commercial use (merchandise, prints, etc.) is priced separately — please inquire.
  • Please allow 1–3 weeks for completion, depending on complexity and queue.
  • I reserve the right to decline commissions that don't fit my style or comfort level.
