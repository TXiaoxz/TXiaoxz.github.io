Medical Imaging Active @ JHU

Multimodal MRI-MRA Registration for Trigeminal Neuralgia

Developing multimodal registration pipelines to support vessel localization in trigeminal neuralgia, with explicit evaluation beyond whole-brain metrics.

  • ROI-focused overlap, intensity-similarity, and distance-based metrics
  • Failure-case analysis for vessel-level misalignment
  • Exploration of manual labels, pseudo-labels, and vessel-aware priors
Segmentation Pipeline Work

Neurovascular Segmentation and Vessel Localization Pipeline

Building preprocessing and quality-control pipelines for 3D neuroimaging data, then integrating vessel segmentation outputs into downstream analysis.

  • Masking, cropping, label conversion, and image-space standardization
  • Automated processing scripts for reproducible experiments
  • Alignment with expert-annotated vessel regions for analysis
