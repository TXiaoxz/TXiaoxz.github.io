How I got here

I moved to the US in middle school with very little English. I couldn't follow class, my grades showed it, and for a while I kept to myself. I started high school with a 1.9 GPA and a warning that I might be asked to leave.

Mr. Hilton, my high school choir teacher, noticed I could sing and brought me into the choir. He also taught AP Computer Science. I spent the next three years in both of his rooms. I sang first tenor and led the section, and I took four AP courses my senior year and got an A in every one.

I kept singing in college — not leading anymore, but I never stopped. Singing and computer science both started in the same place, with the same teacher.

Thank you, Mr. Hilton.
